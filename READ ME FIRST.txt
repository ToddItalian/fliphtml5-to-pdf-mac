====================================================
FlipHTML5 to PDF (macOS One-Click App)
====================================================

WHAT IT DOES:
Converts public FlipHTML5 books into clean, multi-page PDFs saved directly to your Desktop—with a live progress bar and automatic cleanup. Uses only built-in macOS tools (no Python, Node.js, or extra installs needed).

----------------------------------------------------
ONE-TIME SETUP (TAKES 30 SECONDS)
----------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Enable Safari Apple Events (Required Once)
1. Open Safari.
2. In the top menu bar, click Safari > Settings... (or press Cmd + ,).
3. Go to the "Advanced" tab and check "Show features for web developers".
4. In the top menu bar, click Develop > Allow JavaScript from Apple Events (confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID).

STEP 2: Bypass the Unidentified Developer Warning (First Launch Only)
Because this is an independent free utility not signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate, macOS will block a normal double-click the first time:
1. Drag "FlipHTML5_Downloader.app" to your Desktop or Applications folder.
2. Right-click (or Control-click) the app icon and click "Open".
3. Click "Open" again in the popup confirmation window.
(After doing this once, a normal double-click will always work.)

----------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE
----------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "FlipHTML5_Downloader.app".
2. Paste any public FlipHTML5 link and click "Download PDF".
3. Safari will briefly open and close the link to read the page list, then a progress bar will show each page downloading and converting.
4. When finished, your titled PDF will appear right on your Desktop.
